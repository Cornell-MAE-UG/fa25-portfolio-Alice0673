## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/photoshopped-headshot.png" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

 
My name is {{ site.name }}, and I am a Mechanical Engineering student at Cornell University focused on propulsion systems, computational modeling, and performance-driven design. My work spans physics-based N-body simulations of electrospray plumes, high-speed experimental flow visualization, and electromagnetic system design for linear induction propulsion.

I am particularly interested in problems where physical modeling, numerical simulation, and hardware constraints intersect. Whether validating particle interaction solvers, extracting quantitative metrics from high-speed imaging, or designing mechanical systems under real-world constraints, I aim to build solutions that are both analytically rigorous and practically grounded.

Through research and design projects, I have developed experience in C++ scientific codebases, Python-based data visualization, CAD and electromagnetic simulation, and structured experimental validation. I am motivated by systems that demand precision, performance, and reliability—especially in aerospace and high-energy environments.
